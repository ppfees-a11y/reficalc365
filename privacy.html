Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RefiCalc365 handles information when you use its calculators and educational pages.

We do not intentionally transmit your calculator inputs to our server. Your browser and network environment may still process normal technical information required to load the site.

For details about Google’s data practices and advertising choices, visit Google’s advertising technology information. You can also review Google My Ad Center to manage ad preferences.

Hosting and delivery providers may collect routine technical information, such as IP address, device information, requested URLs and timestamps, for security and operations. We do not claim control over those providers’ independent data practices.

Our educational pages may link to external resources. Their privacy policies apply when you visit those websites.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the site or its advertising practices change. The version published on this page is the current website policy.
